# The Blanket of Bullets
**Source:** Miracle of Love / By His Grace by Dada Mukerjee & Ram Dass  
**Category:** Protection Across Distance | **Location:** Bhavania Ashram, World War II  
**Tags:** war, bullet, soldier, blanket, protection, miracle

## Summary
During WWII, a devotee's son was deployed on the battlefield. At night in India, Baba wrapped himself tightly in his heavy wool blanket, groaning in severe pain as if taking bullet hits. In the morning, Baba tossed the blanket down, and heavy lead bullets rolled onto the floor while Baba remained unscathed. A letter later arrived confirming the son had survived direct gunfire.

## Full Story
During World War II, an Indian devotee couple had their only son serving on the dangerous frontlines overseas. One evening at Bhavania Ashram, Maharaj-ji suddenly lay down, pulled his heavy woolen blanket (kambal) completely over his head, and began groaning, tossing, and sweating through the night as if enduring intense mortar fire and agony. The devotees sat outside terrified, not daring to disturb him. At dawn, Baba threw the blanket aside and shook it out—heavy spent lead bullets cascaded onto the floor with a metallic clatter. Baba laughed and said, 'The boy was in great danger, but he is safe now.' Weeks later, a letter arrived from the front: the son wrote that his entire company had been ambushed and wiped out by machine-gun fire, but an invisible armor had shielded his chest, deflecting every bullet harmlessly away.

## Spiritual Lesson
A saint's grace is not bound by space or physical distance. Sincere devotion bridges thousands of miles instantly.
