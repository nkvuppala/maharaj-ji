# Dr. Richard Alpert and His Mother's Spleen
**Source:** Be Here Now / Miracle of Love by Ram Dass  
**Category:** Omniscience | **Location:** Kainchi / Nainital hills, 1967  
**Tags:** ram dass, richard alpert, mind reading, mother, grief

## Summary
Harvard psychologist Dr. Richard Alpert sat before Maharaj-ji as a staunch Western skeptic. Baba looked into his eyes and asked why he had been thinking about his deceased mother and the spleen disease that killed her the previous night under the stars miles away. Alpert broke down in tears, dissolved his intellectual ego, and was reborn as Ram Dass.

## Full Story
When Harvard psychologist Dr. Richard Alpert first traveled into the Himalayan foothills to meet Maharaj-ji, he was full of Western scientific skepticism. The previous night, sitting alone in the dark miles away in an apple orchard, Alpert had looked at the stars and thought tenderly of his mother, who had recently passed away in Boston from a disease of the spleen. He had told nobody about this intimate thought. The next morning, upon arriving at the ashram, Maharaj-ji looked right through him, smiled warmly, and asked in Hindi: 'You were thinking about your mother last night, weren't you? She died of spleen, yes?' Alpert felt as if an invisible laser had pierced through every defense mechanism of his psyche. In a single instant, his academic cynicism melted into uncontrollable weeping, experiencing unconditional love for the first time. He was rechristened 'Ram Dass' (Servant of God).

## Spiritual Lesson
The true guru knows every hidden chamber of your heart before you even utter a word, meeting you with boundless love.
