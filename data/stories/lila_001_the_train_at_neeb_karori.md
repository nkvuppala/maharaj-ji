# The Train at Neeb Karori
**Source:** Miracle of Love by Ram Dass  
**Category:** Mastery Over Nature | **Location:** Neeb Karori Railway Station, Uttar Pradesh  
**Tags:** train, railway, humility, miracle, conductor

## Summary
Maharaj-ji boarded a first-class compartment without a ticket. A conductor kicked him off in the rural stop of Neeb Karori. When the driver tried to start the train, the locomotive refused to move despite full steam pressure. Only after the station master and conductor begged Baba to reboard did the train move forward effortlessly.

## Full Story
In the early days, Maharaj-ji boarded a first-class train compartment. At the rural village of Neeb Karori, an ticket collector discovered he had no ticket and rudely forced Baba off the train into the dusty heat. Baba sat quietly under a tree nearby, sticking his tongs (chimta) into the dirt. When the green flag waved, the driver opened the throttle, but the train would not budge an inch. They brought another locomotive to pull it, but that failed too. A local station master recognized Baba and warned the railway officials: 'You have insulted a great saint. Until he returns, this train will not move.' The ticket collector and conductor ran to Baba, apologized with folded hands, and begged him to return. Baba laughed playfully, agreed on two conditions—that the railway build a proper station for the poor villagers of Neeb Karori and that ticket collectors treat poor wanderers with kindness. When Baba stepped back on board, the train immediately surged forward without effort.

## Spiritual Lesson
Outer pride and authority crumble before pure spiritual innocence. Treat every wandering soul with kindness.
