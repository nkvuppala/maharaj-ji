# Larry Brilliant and the WHO Smallpox Mission
**Source:** Sometimes Brilliant / Miracle of Love by Dr. Larry Brilliant  
**Category:** Divine Mission & Seva | **Location:** Kainchi Dham, 1970s  
**Tags:** larry brilliant, smallpox, who, eradication, doctor, seva

## Summary
Young American doctor Larry Brilliant wanted to meditate in the ashram in quiet contemplation. Baba poked him and ordered him to go work for the United Nations World Health Organization to eradicate smallpox. Despite having no contacts, Larry was hired, and smallpox was successfully wiped out from India.

## Full Story
Dr. Larry Brilliant had left his medical career in America to seek enlightenment, sitting peacefully under the trees of Kainchi Dham in white robes. One afternoon, Maharaj-ji summoned him, tapped him firmly on the ribs, and said loudly: 'Doctor, why are you wasting your time sitting here with your eyes closed? Go to New Delhi and work for the World Health Organization. You are going to eradicate smallpox from India! This is God's gift to suffering humanity.' Larry was bewildered; he had no money, no connections at the UN, and smallpox had plagued humanity for three thousand years. He went to Delhi and was rejected repeatedly, but kept returning because Baba insisted. Eventually, an opening appeared; Larry joined the WHO team as a key leader, and within a few years, India celebrated its very last case of smallpox. Millions of lives were spared.

## Spiritual Lesson
True spirituality is not escaping the world into passive trance; it is dedicating your skills to the practical alleviation of human suffering.
