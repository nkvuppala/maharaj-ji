# Feeding the Abusive Man with Sweets
**Source:** By His Grace by Dada Mukerjee  
**Category:** Forgiveness & Love | **Location:** Allahabad  
**Tags:** forgiveness, anger, puris, sweets, love, enemy

## Summary
A man filled with bitter hatred arrived at the house where Baba was staying, shouting insults and accusations. Instead of showing anger, Baba welcomed him with open arms, fed him rich sweets, and treated him like a long-lost son. The man broke into tears and prostrated in repentance.

## Full Story
In Allahabad, a local man had become fiercely jealous of Maharaj-ji's growing reputation and arrived outside the house shouting angry slanders and harsh abuses. The devotees rushed to shut the gate and call for help, but Maharaj-ji stopped them instantly: 'Do not touch him! Bring him inside immediately.' When the red-faced man was ushered into the room, Baba stood up, wrapped him in a blanket, and shouted to the kitchen: 'Quickly! Bring hot puris, kachoris, and jalebis! My son has come from so far and he is exhausted and hungry!' Baba fed the man with his own hands, patting his back with tender affection. Disarmed by pure warmth where he expected retaliation, the man collapsed at Baba's feet, weeping bitterly: 'Baba, I came to curse you, and you feed me like a mother.' Baba stroked his head: 'Everyone is suffering, beta. Anger is only pain shouting for love.'

## Spiritual Lesson
Never fight hatred with anger. Love and nourishment disarm the darkest hostility.
