# Planting Seeds in Every Soul
**Source:** Miracle of Love by Ram Dass  
**Category:** Wisdom & Pedagogy | **Location:** Vrindavan Ashram  
**Tags:** seeds, faith, transformation, compassion

## Summary
Ram Dass once questioned Baba for praising and giving blessings to worldly, hypocritical visitors. Baba explained that by telling someone they are a great devotee, he plants a divine seed in their heart that will eventually blossom.

## Full Story
One afternoon at Vrindavan, a prominent politician known for dishonesty and corruption visited the ashram. To Ram Dass's dismay, Maharaj-ji greeted the politician warmly, praised him, and placed a garland around his neck, calling him a 'great devotee of Lord Ram.' Later, when the visitor departed, Ram Dass expressed his confusion: 'Maharaj-ji, that man is known to be corrupt! Why did you tell him he is a great bhakta?' Baba looked at Ram Dass with profound gentleness: 'Ram Dass, if I tell a man he is bad, he will become worse. But if I tell him he is a devotee of God, I have planted a sacred seed in his consciousness. Day and night, he will wonder: Can I become worthy of what Baba saw in me? If even ten out of a hundred people change their life from that seed, the world becomes a better place.'

## Spiritual Lesson
Always address the highest divinity in others, never their lowest flaws. Seeing the good in people inspires them to rise toward it.
