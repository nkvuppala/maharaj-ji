# The Mysterious Car with an Empty Tank
**Source:** Miracle of Love by Ram Dass  
**Category:** Mastery Over Nature | **Location:** Himalayan Foothills  
**Tags:** car, petrol, night, mountains, journey

## Summary
A devotee was driving Maharaj-ji late at night through a deserted mountain road when the petrol gauge dropped to dead empty with miles to go. Baba patted the dashboard and commanded the driver to keep driving. The car drove for hours over mountain passes until reaching safety.

## Full Story
Late one evening, a devotee named Mr. Tewari was chauffeuring Maharaj-ji through a desolate mountain stretch between Bareilly and Almora. Suddenly, the engine spluttered and the fuel gauge pointer dropped below empty. There was not a petrol station for thirty miles, and the road was pitch dark and freezing cold. The driver panicked: 'Maharaj-ji, the petrol is finished! We are stranded on this mountain pass.' Baba hit the dashboard with his hand and laughed heartily: 'Drive, you foolish man! Don't look at the dial, look at the road!' The driver stepped on the accelerator, and the car hummed smoothly. They drove for nearly forty-five miles across steep mountain climbs. When they finally rolled into a town station the next morning, the mechanic removed the fuel cap and tapped the tank—it was bone-dry. The car had traveled on faith and Baba's word alone.

## Spiritual Lesson
When you surrender anxiety and focus purely on your duty, divine grace carries you far past human limitation.
