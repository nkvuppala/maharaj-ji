# Never Throw Banana Peels
**Source:** Miracle of Love by Ram Dass  
**Category:** Ecology & Respect | **Location:** Kainchi Dham  
**Tags:** banana peel, waste, nature, sub ek, reverence

## Summary
When a devotee casually tossed a banana peel onto the ground, Baba immediately stopped him and reminded everyone that nothing in God's creation is waste, for all of life is divine and interconnected.

## Full Story
A devotee was eating a banana near Maharaj-ji's wooden takhat and casually threw the skin onto the path. Maharaj-ji immediately leaned forward with a look of genuine concern: 'Why did you throw that away like that? Pick it up! Even a cow, a bird, or an ant can take nourishment from it. Nothing in God's creation is waste. See God in everything, even a banana peel.' The devotee gathered the peel and placed it respectfully near a feeding trough where a wandering cow ate it moments later. Baba used everyday simplicity to teach profound non-dual Vedanta: that there is no 'sacred' separate from 'ordinary'—everything belongs to the Divine.

## Spiritual Lesson
Respect every scrap of creation. When you live with conscious reverence, no resource is wasted and no living creature is neglected.
