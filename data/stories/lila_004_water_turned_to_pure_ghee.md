# Water Turned to Pure Ghee
**Source:** Miracle of Love by Ram Dass  
**Category:** Mastery Over Nature | **Location:** Kainchi Dham Ashram  
**Tags:** kainchi, ghee, river, cooking, bhandara, prasad

## Summary
During a massive festival (bhandara) at Kainchi Dham, the cooks ran out of clarified butter (ghee) to fry puris for the waiting crowd. Baba instructed them to scoop buckets of cold water from the nearby river and pour it straight into the hot frying cauldrons. The water miraculously turned into pure, fragrant ghee.

## Full Story
At a major festival in Kainchi Dham, thousands of pilgrims arrived unexpectedly from the surrounding mountain villages to receive darshan and prasad. The ashram kitchen was preparing mountains of puris, but halfway through the day, the cooks rushed to Maharaj-ji in distress: 'Baba, all the tins of ghee are empty! We have hundreds of hungry people waiting and no money or market open nearby.' Baba scolded them playfully: 'Why do you worry? Go take two large canisters down to the river, fill them with water, and pour it into the hot frying pans.' The cooks hesitated, fearing boiling oil and water would explode, but obeyed out of faith. When the river water touched the giant iron pans, it turned into sweet, fragrant golden ghee. The next morning, when new tins of ghee arrived from the market, Baba instructed: 'Now take two tins of ghee and pour them back into the river to repay Mother Ganga.'

## Spiritual Lesson
When service is done selflessly for God's children, the divine provides abundance out of thin air, but always honor nature with gratitude.
