# Taking on the Devotee's Illness
**Source:** By His Grace by Dada Mukerjee  
**Category:** Compassion & Sacrifice | **Location:** Allahabad  
**Tags:** illness, sacrifice, health, fever, dada

## Summary
When a devotee fell critically ill with high fever, Baba would often begin shivering and running a high temperature himself. As Baba's fever broke, the devotee's illness vanished, demonstrating the saint taking on the karma of his disciples.

## Full Story
Dada Mukerjee recorded numerous instances where devotees on the brink of death or suffering from agonizing illnesses suddenly recovered after Baba visited them. In Allahabad, an elderly devotee was afflicted with virulent pneumonia; doctors had given up hope. That night, Baba retired to his room, groaning and clutching his chest, coughing violently while his body burned with high fever. His disciples watched helplessly as Baba suffered through the night. By morning, Baba took a bath, drank tea, and was completely healthy. At the same time, the doctor visited the sick devotee and was stunned to find his lungs completely clear and fever gone. When devotees asked Baba about it, he waved his hand dismissively: 'Doctors know nothing. Ram takes care of everything.'

## Spiritual Lesson
The true satguru silently absorbs the suffering of those who call on him with sincere faith.
